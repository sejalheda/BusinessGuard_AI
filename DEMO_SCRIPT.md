# BusinessGuard AI — Demo Video Script (2-3 Minutes)

**Target Duration:** 2 minutes 30 seconds  
**Characters:** Narrator / Presenter, Priya (Instagram Seller)  
**Setting:** Split-screen showing simulated Amazon Echo device and BusinessGuard Web Application  

---

### [0:00 - 0:25] Scene 1: The Crisis (Hook)

**(Visual: Close-up on a smartphone screen showing an Instagram boutique named "Saree Haven". An error pops up: "You have been logged out. Your email has been changed to x99_security@mail.ru".)**

**Narrator (Voiceover):**  
"Meet Priya. She sells sarees on Instagram. Today she lost her account."

"Over the last three years, Priya built an audience of 28,000 customers. This morning, she received a direct message warning her of an urgent copyright infringement. In a panic, she clicked the verification link. Within four minutes, her password was reset, her two-factor authentication was bypassed, and the hacker began posting fraudulent crypto promotions to her followers."

"Priya has no IT department, no corporate lawyer, and Meta support is taking days. Who does a solo entrepreneur call in the first 60 minutes?"

---

### [0:25 - 1:05] Scene 2: The Alexa First Responder Call

**(Visual: Priya taps the glowing cyan Alexa+ Echo Ring on the BusinessGuard console.)**

**Priya (Speaking into microphone):**  
*"Alexa, ask BusinessGuard, my Instagram was hacked."*

**(Visual: The Echo ring pulses bright cyan into thinking mode, then breathes emerald green as Alexa speaks.)**

**Alexa (Audio Playback):**  
*"I understand how urgent this is. Can you still log in to your Instagram account on your mobile app?"*

**Priya:**  
*"No, my password was changed."*

**Alexa:**  
*"Did you receive an official security email from Instagram saying your email address or phone number was changed?"*

**Priya:**  
*"Yes, I got an email saying my email was changed to something else."*

**Alexa:**  
*"Are the attackers currently posting fake stories or messaging your followers for money?"*

**Priya:**  
*"Yes, they are asking followers to invest in crypto right now!"*

**(Visual: The Alexa ring completes the analysis and generates a Case ID.)**

**Alexa (Voiceover):**  
*"I have registered Case BG-2026-005. Here are your first three immediate actions:  
One: Search your registered email for an alert from security at mail dot instagram dot com and tap 'Revert This Change'.  
Two: On your Instagram app login screen, tap 'Need More Help' and submit a Video Selfie Identity Verification.  
Three: Post an emergency warning on your personal WhatsApp status telling customers not to transfer funds.  
You can now continue this exact case on your BusinessGuard web app using Case ID BG-2026-005."*

### [1:05 - 1:40] Scene 3: The Seamless Web App Handoff

**(Visual: Screen transitions to the BusinessGuard Web App. The top bar enters Case ID BG-2026-005. The app shifts to a dramatic red emergency theme with a pulsing alert.)**

**Narrator:**  
"This is BusinessGuard's signature handoff. The owner starts a rescue by voice on Alexa, gets a Case ID, and immediately picks up the exact same case on screen."

**Narrator:**  
"Notice the top banner: 'Continued from Alexa Voice Rescue'. All voice dialogue turns, answers, and timestamps were persisted directly through our self-hosted Model Context Protocol (MCP) server into local SQLite. No context lost, zero duplicate questions."

"The system automatically switches into Emergency Mode with a 4-part containment dossier:  
1. **Immediate Actions (0-60 min)** to stop follower scamming.  
2. **A 24-Hour Plan** to unlink Meta Business Suite ad accounts.  
3. **An Evidence Checklist** for filing reports on cybercrime.gov.in.  
4. **Future Hardening** steps."
